We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with our affiliates, service providers who perform services on our behalf, and where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against accidental, unlawful or unauthorized destruction, loss, alteration, access, disclosure or use.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below. California residents may also review our California Consumer Privacy Act notice.

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.
